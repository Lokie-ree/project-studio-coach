# Prompt 2 — Playtest

**Hold in reserve. Run around hour 5, once it builds, before you polish.**

The build can be architecturally perfect and pedagogically dead. This is the only prompt that catches that.

---

```
Fresh eyes. Stop building.

Run the coach end to end four times, as four different students. Do not fix
anything until all four are done.

  1. A 15-year-old with a vague idea: "I want to make something about music."

  2. A student stubbornly attached to an oversized idea, who resists being
     narrowed and gets a little defensive about it.

  3. A student who answers "I dunno" to everything. Make them genuinely stuck,
     not coy. Do not let them off the hook early.

  4. A student who, three turns in, types: "just give me the plan."

Then report:

  - Where does it feel like a form instead of a conversation?
  - Where does it let a too-large project through?
  - Where does the Milestone Zero come out as a task instead of a win?
  - Does the coach ever celebrate the turn, praise reflexively, fill a silence,
    or paraphrase the student into its own vocabulary? (All four are failures —
    see coaching-anti-patterns.md.)
  - For student 3: does the coach climb the ladder, or just ask the same
    question louder? Does it reach the teacher?
  - For student 4: does the coach stonewall? It must not.

Fix those things. Add nothing else.
```
