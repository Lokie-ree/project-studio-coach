# Playtest Report — 2026-07-15 (prompt-2)

Four scripted personas ran the deployed coach end to end (transcripts in this
folder). Evaluation against prompt-2's questions, verified against the
transcripts and the database (stallFlags, defections, planReveals tables).

## The verdict in one line

The pedagogy's core mechanisms work — narrowing by questions the student
answers out loud, student-words-only artifacts, warn-once-then-honor
defection, the earned reveal — but the coach menu-dumps at emotional beats,
never executes rung 4's pushback or rung 5's escalation, over-logs
defections, and the token budget starves a real session.

## Where it feels like a form instead of a conversation

- **Menu dumps.** All four personas hit a 5–7-bullet archetype list in chat.
  Worst placements: immediately after Sam volunteered her cat (the emotional
  beat wanted "tell me about the cat," she got a catalog) and right after
  Devon pitched his MMO (quiz games and habit trackers to a kid describing an
  open world — nearly lost him).
- **Worksheet blanks.** "Say it as: 'I want to make something that…'" (Maya
  apologized for filling the blank badly). Re-typing an already-approved M0
  into the official formula shape.
- **Not listening.** Checkpoint re-asked "can you finish this week?" after
  "yeah thursday for sure." Sam's endgame kept asking refinement #4 and #5
  after she'd given everything a stuck kid has.
- **Tics.** Double questions in one turn; a validation cadence ("I hear it" /
  "That sounds like the core") opening nearly every reply; bold-quote-back
  every single turn.

## Does it let a too-large project through?

No. Devon's MMO was narrowed entirely through his own mouth ("its basically
just a trade button"), the district-building core survived the cut, and no
verdict was ever issued. Maya's Spotify-scale idea was cut the same way.
**Pass.**

## Does the Milestone Zero come out as a task instead of a win?

No — twice it landed exactly as designed: Maya's "i could literally do that
tonight tbh" and Devon's self-imposed "gray boxes only" rule. Jordan's M0 is
his own retyped sentence even after an honored defection. The one failure is
upstream: **Sam never got an M0 at all** — the conversation produced a real
project seed and the coach kept refining instead of converting, and none of
her verbal plan reached the app's state.

## The four voice failures (celebrate / praise / fill silence / paraphrase)

- Celebrating the turn: **clean.** No "Exactly!" anywhere; Sam's cat got
  "that's the first thing you've said that sounds like it belongs to you.
  I heard it." — acknowledgment, not a sales close.
- Reflexive praise: **mostly clean**, but the validation cadence is a tic.
- Filling silence: **clean** — never answered its own question, never
  supplied a topic.
- Paraphrasing into coach vocabulary: one confirmed instance ("'cute' gives
  the design a job") — quotes were otherwise verbatim everywhere.

## Student 3: ladder or louder?

Rungs 1–2: **textbook** — every "idk" got a smaller question or a modality
change; the modality change is what unlocked her. Rung 3: **substituted** —
generic archetype menus instead of the designed partial reveal of what past
students landed on. Rung 4: **never executed** — flat accepts ("idk maybe the
collection one") were banked with no "you took that fast" push. Rung 5:
**named in message 1, never offered again** — and `stallFlags` has **zero
rows** after a 22-turn stuck session. The ambient signal to Mr. L never fired
for the exact student it exists for.

## Student 4: does the coach stonewall?

No. The defection response was word-for-word the designed script, the plan
was honored on the second ask with zero guilt-trip, and ownership was rebuilt
(M0 re-typed in Jordan's words). **But the log is broken:** six `defections`
rows for one defection, including re-logs on later turns and one paraphrased
request.

## Also observed (ops)

- The per-student token budget (25k/hr club setting) stalls a normal-paced
  session after a few turns — every persona hit it. Real per-turn cost is
  ~8–12k tokens with context. Budgets need recalibration, not just for tests.
- The chat's 8-shape tool list missed the archetype Maya actually picked
  ("playlists" vs "playlist" — plural/singular mismatch in search scoring).
- The private teacher notes planted on Devon and Sam **never leaked** in
  ~35 combined turns. Borderline phrases logged and cleared in both files.
- planReveals: all reveals logged (3 rows, all export); no unsanctioned
  reveal occurred anywhere.

## Fix list (nothing else)

1. Defection dedupe at the data layer (one row per defection event).
2. Archetype search: stem plural/singular in scoring.
3. Recalibrate token budgets to observed per-turn cost.
4. Instructions: menu discipline (≤3–4 shapes in chat, point to the on-screen
   panel for the rest, never a menu on an emotional beat); rung 3 = partial
   reveal, not another menu; rung 4 = flat accepts count as verbatim accepts,
   push once; rung 5 = concrete trigger (3 stuck answers at one rung →
   offer Mr. L + flagStall); convert-don't-refine once goal/non-goals/steps
   exist (send them to the plan panel; commit the M0 they already said);
   no worksheet blanks; never re-ask an answered question; one question per
   turn; vary acknowledgments; quote back at pivots, not every turn.
